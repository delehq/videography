The Art
Of Noticing

I’m Thomas Mamfredas—filmmaker and photographer based in Zakynthos, crafting wedding and commercial films with a calm, detail-driven approach. The work is quiet when it needs to be, honest when it matters, and always focused on story.

From intimate vows to brand narratives, I look for the small truths: light on skin, a hand squeeze, the breath before a line. Fewer, better choices. Images that feel like memories, not performances.
